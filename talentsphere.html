🌍 Project Overview

TalentSphere is a complete AI-powered recruitment and interview platform developed to improve both candidate and recruiter experiences.

The platform combines Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning, Computer Vision and Speech Processing to automate several stages of the recruitment process.

The objective is to help candidates improve their employability while helping recruiters identify and evaluate candidates more efficiently.

The platform includes two main spaces:

👤 Candidate Space

• CV upload and analysis
• AI interview simulation
• Skills assessment quizzes
• Job recommendations
• Personalized CV improvement suggestions

🏢 Recruiter Space

• Automated candidate matching
• Video interviews
• Behavioral analysis
• Voice and emotion analysis
• Automatic recruitment reports

🎯 My Role

Lead — CV Analysis & Guidance Module

Within a team of 6 data scientists and developers, I was responsible for the design, development and deployment of the CV Analysis & Guidance Module.

The objective was to develop an intelligent system capable of understanding the semantic structure of CVs, identifying strengths and weaknesses, and generating personalized improvement suggestions.

My Technical Approach

1. Data Preparation
Processing of the UpdatedResumeDataSet dataset from Kaggle using an 80/20 train-test split.

2. NLP Preprocessing
Data cleaning, tokenization, text preprocessing and extraction of relevant CV information.

3. Transformer Model
Fine-tuning of the RoBERTa Transformer model to capture the semantic structure of CVs and improve classification and analysis.

4. Feedback Generation
Development of a module transforming model predictions into understandable and actionable recommendations for candidates.

📊 Results & Performance

93.48%

Model precision achieved by the RoBERTa-based CV analysis module.

Real-Time Analysis

CV analysis integrated into the React interface through a Flask API.

Personalized Feedback

Candidates receive actionable recommendations to improve their CV.

🧠 Skills Demonstrated

NLP & Transformers: Fine-tuning of Transformer models such as RoBERTa for semantic understanding of CVs.

Data Engineering: Data cleaning, tokenization and preprocessing pipelines for complex textual data.

Model Deployment: Deployment of the fine-tuned model through a REST API using Flask.

Product Development: Transformation of machine learning predictions into useful and understandable candidate feedback.
