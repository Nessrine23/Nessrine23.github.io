🌍 1. The Project

TalentSphere is a web-based full-stack platform designed to digitalize and optimize the entire recruitment cycle. The platform addresses two major challenges: recruiters receive a large number of applications, while candidates often lack personalized feedback about their CVs and interview performance.

To address these challenges, TalentSphere combines Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning and multimodal analysis.

👤 Candidate Space

The candidate can upload a CV, simulate an interview with an AI chatbot, take skill assessment quizzes and receive intelligent job recommendations.

🏢 Recruiter Space

Recruiters can manage applications, automatically match candidates with job offers, conduct video interviews and generate recruitment reports.

🎯 3. My Role

Lead — CV Analysis & Guidance Module

Within a team of six Data Scientists and developers, I was responsible for the design, development and deployment of the CV Analysis & Guidance Module .

💡 The Challenge

The objective was to develop an intelligent engine capable of understanding the semantic structure of a CV, identifying strengths and weaknesses and generating personalized improvement recommendations.

🔬 My Technical Approach

  1. Data Preparation

    Processing of the UpdatedResumeDataSet dataset from Kaggle using an 80/20 split. A preprocessing pipeline was developed for cleaning, tokenization and structural extraction.

  2. Transformer Model

    A Transformer-based architecture was selected. I fine-tuned RoBERTa to capture the semantic structure of CVs and evaluate the relevance of skills.

  3. Feedback Generation

    A conversion module was developed to transform model predictions into understandable and actionable recommendations for candidates.
